About TechNova

We are a software company from Lahore. Since 2019 we have helped more than 120 organisations launch fast and secure products.

Our Story

TechNova started when two engineers saw good ideas fail because software projects were slow and confusing. We decided to do it differently with small teams, clear prices and regular demos.

Today our designers, engineers and security experts work with start-ups, schools and shops. Our mission is to make good technology available to every business.
